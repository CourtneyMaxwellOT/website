Personalized Occupational Therapy & Accessibility Consulting

I help adults and children regain independence, return to meaningful activities, and design inclusive environments — combining clinical best practice with pragmatic accessibility solutions.

I am licensed with the National Board For Certification in Occupational Therapy (NBCOT), with a license to practice in Massachusetts.

About

Hi — I’m Courtney Maxwell, OTR/L. I specialize in adult and pediatric rehabilitation, sensory strategies, assistive technology recommendations, and accessibility consulting for workplaces and education settings. I take a collaborative, evidence-based approach and tailor plans to each person's goals and context.

Approach

My practice emphasizes functional outcomes, shared decision-making, and measurable goals. I partner with families, educators, employers, and interdisciplinary teams to remove barriers and enable participation.
